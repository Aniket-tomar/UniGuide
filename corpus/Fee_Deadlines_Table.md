| Policy Event | Deadline | Refund Percentage | Late Penalty |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Pre-Term Settlement** | 14 calendar days prior to semester commencement | 100% tuition & laboratory fees (less $100 administrative charge) | Nil (standard processing window) |
| **Orientation Week Cancellation** | 23:59 hours prior to official Orientation Day commencement | 100% accommodation and tuition charges | $150 late cancellation assessment |
| **Early Withdrawal** | Within 15 calendar days of semester start | 80% tuition refund | Forfeiture of registration deposit; $250 processing surcharge |
| **Standard Term Withdrawal** | Instructional Days 16 through 30 | 50% tuition refund only (amenity and lab fees retained) | $350 administrative separation fee |
| **Mid-Term Discontinuation** | Instructional Day 31 through end of Week 8 | 0% refund (W mark entered on permanent record) | Full liability for unremitted semester balance |
| **Late Disciplinary Separation** | Subsequent to Week 8 through end of term | 0% refund (F grade permanently assigned) | Immediate acceleration of entire outstanding fee schedule |
| **Supplementary Exam Registration** | 10 days prior to summer examination session | 0% refund upon entry validation | $100 per paper late registration penalty |
