# Planted Contradictions in Corpus

This document outlines the three deliberate contradictions planted across the university corpus to evaluate the AI's conflict-resolution state.

### 1. The Exam Attendance Threshold
- **Clause A (regulations.md - Sec 3.1.2 & 8.4.1):** Mandates 75% attendance, but explicitly allows the Academic Dean to condone attendance down to 60% for medical reasons.
- **Clause B (hostel_policy.pdf - Sec 11.3):** Explicitly strips the Dean of this power, stating no one can lower the 75% threshold under any circumstance.

### 2. The Grade Improvement Rule
- **Clause A (regulations.md - Sec 5.1):** Allows students with a 'D' or 'F' grade to sit for supplementary improvement exams.
- **Clause B (hostel_policy.pdf - Sec 9.3):** Prohibits 'D' grade students from taking the exam, reserving it strictly for 'F' grades.

### 3. The Tuition Refund Window
- **Clause A (fee_schedule.md - Table Row 3):** Guarantees an 80% tuition refund if a student withdraws within 15 days of the semester starting.
- **Clause B (hostel_policy.pdf - Sec 2.4):** States tuition is 100% non-refundable the moment orientation day begins, regardless of withdrawal date.
